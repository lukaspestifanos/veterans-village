Get help

Getting started is simple.

Every veteran and senior's situation is different, so we start with a conversation. There is no wrong way to reach us.

  1. Call or send us a message

    Call (253) 257-7804 during office hours or use the short form on this page. Tell us a little about your situation.

  2. We talk through what you need

    A team member will go over housing options, eligibility, and the services that fit, whether that is counseling, food assistance, medical care, or job training.

  3. We connect you to the right place

    We match you with a community that has availability, or with a trusted partner organization if we cannot help directly.

We operate two kinds of communities today, with more under construction. Pick the one that fits your situation, then call the property or ask us to help.

Family apartments

Studio to four-bedroom homes with in-unit laundry, modern kitchens, and community amenities. Quinn by Vintage, Two10 by Vintage, and Silver Creek.

    Our mission

    Supported and respected, from the first day in a new home.

    "Our mission is to ensure that our veterans and seniors are supported and respected while they transition into their new homes, through affordable housing, behavioral health counseling, and other necessary services."Veterans Village mission statement

    Over the years, Veterans Village has had a positive impact on the lives of thousands of veterans and their families. We are proud of the difference we have made and committed to continuing the work.

    • Reliability

      A trustworthy partner to veterans, their families, and our community partners.

    • Self-sufficiency

      Opportunities for veterans and their families to build independent, stable lives.

    • Advocacy

      We advocate for the rights and well-being of the veterans and seniors we serve.

    • Partnership

      We work closely with other nonprofits to ensure effective, reliable care.

    About

    Founded by veterans, for veterans, and the seniors who served alongside them.

    Veterans Village was founded in 2016 by several veterans who were passionate about helping other veterans and their families. We are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to making a positive difference in their lives.

    We partner with other nonprofit organizations to make sure our services are effective and reliable, and that the veterans we serve receive the best possible care. Our office is in Spanaway, Washington.

    Leadership

    Board of Directors
